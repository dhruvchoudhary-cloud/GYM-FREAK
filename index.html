FAQ

Questions, Answered.

We're open Monday through Saturday, 5:00 AM – 9:00 PM. We're closed on Sundays so the arena stays fresh and well-maintained for the week ahead.

We offer Starter, Elite, and Pro memberships across monthly, quarterly, and yearly billing. Every plan includes full access to the gym floor, cardio, and free-weight zones.

On-site parking is available for both two-wheelers and cars right outside the arena on Rohta Road.

Our certified trainers are available for one-on-one coaching. Elite and Pro members get sessions included; Starter members can add sessions individually.

Diet consultation is included with Elite and Pro memberships, with fully custom programming for Pro members based on your training goals.

Sessions can be booked directly at the front desk or through our WhatsApp line — just let us know your preferred trainer and time slot.
